Welcome! I am a Research Fellow in Economics at <a href = "https://www.qub.ac.uk/schools/queens-business-school/research/economics/" target="_blank">Queen's University Belfast</a>. Previously, I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher and Lecturer in Economic, Social and Demographic History at <a href = "https://www.ru.nl/en/departments/department-of-history-art-history-and-classics/economical-social-and-demographic-history" target="_blank">Radboud University</a>. 

My research studies the causes and consequences of social inequalities in the past and present, covering the labour market, education, and health. My <a href = "https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item%3A4261318/view" target="_blank">PhD thesis</a> examined the social mobility and integration of Amsterdam Jews 1850-1940, with special attention for the ethnic niche of the diamond industry. 

I am also Chair of the <a href = "https://younghistoricaldemographers.com" target="_blank">Association for Young Historical Demographers</a>.
