## 🌱 Welcome!
I am a Research Fellow in Economics at <a href = "https://www.qub.ac.uk/schools/queens-business-school/research/economics/" target="_blank">Queen's University Belfast</a>. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher and Lecturer in Economic, Social and Demographic History at <a href = "https://www.ru.nl/en/departments/department-of-history-art-history-and-classics/economical-social-and-demographic-history" target="_blank">Radboud University</a>, and Junior Data Engineer and Doctoral Researcher at the <a href = "https://iisg.amsterdam/en" target="_blank">International Institute of Social History</a>. 

My research examines the causes and consequences of social inequalities in the past and present, particularly in relation to work, education, and health. My <a href = "https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item%3A4261318/view" target="_blank">PhD thesis</a> analysed the social mobility and integration of Jews in Amsterdam between 1850 and 1940, with special attention to the economic niche of the diamond industry. In ongoing work, I study gender gaps and social inequalities in cause-specific adult mortality, focusing on infectious disease mortality in nineteenth-century Amsterdam. My current research examines the impact of indoor and outdoor air pollution on health on the island of Ireland as part of the <a href = "https://peace-air.eu" target="_blank">PEACE-Air project</a>.

In addition, I also serve as Chair of the <a href = "https://younghistoricaldemographers.com" target="_blank">Association for Young Historical Demographers</a>, a volunteer organisation that organises events and disseminates information to early-career researchers in historical demography and related fields.
